Trading Tips for Beginners

Build your knowledge before entering the market.

1. Learn Before You Trade

Understand stocks, indices, market orders, and basic trading concepts before risking real money.

2. Start with a Demo

Practise with virtual money first. This helps you understand how trades work without risking your savings.

3. Make a Trading Plan

Decide your entry price, exit price, risk limit, and reason for taking a trade before placing an order.

4. Manage Your Risk

Never risk money you cannot afford to lose. Avoid borrowing money to trade, and be extra careful with leverage and derivatives.

5. Control Your Emotions

Fear, greed, and revenge trading can lead to poor decisions. Follow your plan instead of chasing losses.

6. Keep a Trading Journal

Record your trade idea, entry, exit, result, and lessons learned. Reviewing your decisions can help you improve over time.

7. Avoid Common Mistakes

  • Trading without learning the basics.
  • Following random tips without research.
  • Investing all your money in one trade.
  • Ignoring transaction costs and risk.
  • Expecting guaranteed profits.

Remember

There is no guaranteed-profit strategy in trading. Focus on learning, discipline, and protecting your capital.
