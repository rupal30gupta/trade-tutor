About AI Trade Tutor

Making financial education simple, accessible and easier to understand for everyone.

Who We Are

AI Trade Tutor is an educational website designed to help beginners understand the stock market and basic trading concepts in simple language.

Our goal is to make financial learning easier through structured lessons, practice questions, educational tools and an AI tutor interface.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to help students and beginners develop financial knowledge and understand how financial markets work.

We believe that learning about risks, investment concepts and financial decisions is an important step towards financial awareness.

🚀 Our Vision

Our vision is to create a beginner-friendly digital learning platform where users can explore finance and trading concepts without feeling overwhelmed by complicated terminology.
