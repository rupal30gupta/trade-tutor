📘 Profit and Loss Formula

Profit or Loss = (Sell Price − Buy Price) × Quantity


Example

Buy Price = ₹100

Sell Price = ₹120

Quantity = 10 shares


Profit = (₹120 − ₹100) × 10

Profit = ₹200

This example excludes brokerage, taxes and other transaction costs.

📊 Percentage Return

Percentage Return = (Profit or Loss ÷ Initial Investment) × 100


In the example above, the initial investment is ₹1,000 and the profit is ₹200.

Percentage Return = (200 ÷ 1,000) × 100

Percentage Return = 20%

Understand the Risk

Actual trading results may differ due to price movements, transaction charges and taxes. Profit is never guaranteed.
