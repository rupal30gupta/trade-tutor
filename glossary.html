Trading Glossary

Understand important stock market terms with simple definitions and examples.

What Is a Trading Glossary?

A trading glossary is a collection of financial words and their meanings. It helps beginners understand common market terminology.

A — Important Terms

Asset

Something with economic value, such as stocks, bonds or property.


Ask Price

The price at which a seller is willing to sell a security.

B — Important Terms

Bear Market

A market condition in which prices are generally falling over a sustained period.


Broker

An intermediary that facilitates the buying and selling of financial securities.


Bull Market

A market condition in which prices are generally rising over a sustained period.

C — Important Terms

Call Option (CE)

An option that gives its buyer the right, but not the obligation, to buy an underlying asset under specified contract terms.


Capital

Money or other resources used for investment or business activities.

D — Important Terms

Derivative

A financial contract whose value depends on an underlying asset or benchmark.


Diversification

Spreading investments across different assets to reduce concentration risk.

I — Important Terms

IPO

Initial Public Offering. It is when a company offers its shares to the public for the first time.


Index

A measure that tracks the performance of a selected group of securities, such as Nifty 50.

M — Important Terms

Market Capitalisation

The total market value of a company's outstanding shares.


Margin

Money or collateral required by a broker or exchange for certain trading positions.

O — Important Terms

Option

A financial contract that gives its buyer a right to buy or sell an underlying asset under specified conditions.

P — Important Terms

Portfolio

A collection of investments held by an individual or institution.


Put Option (PE)

An option that gives its buyer the right, but not the obligation, to sell an underlying asset under specified contract terms.

R — Important Terms

Risk Management

Identifying and managing potential losses when making financial decisions.


Return

The gain or loss from an investment over a particular period.

S — Important Terms

Share

A unit representing ownership in a company.


Stock Exchange

A regulated marketplace where eligible securities are traded.

V — Important Terms

Volatility

The degree to which a market price changes over a period of time.


Volume

The quantity of a security traded during a specified period.
