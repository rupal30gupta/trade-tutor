Welcome to Our Help Centre

Here are some common questions beginners may have while learning about financial markets and using our educational website.

1. What is AI Trade Tutor?

AI Trade Tutor is an educational website designed to help beginners understand stock market concepts, trading terminology and financial calculations.

2. What is the stock market?

The stock market is a marketplace where shares of listed companies can be bought and sold through authorised channels.

3. Can beginners use this website?

Yes. The website is designed to introduce basic financial concepts in simple language for students and beginners.

4. What is the purpose of the AI Tutor?

The AI Tutor section demonstrates how a learning assistant could explain financial concepts and answer educational questions. In this HTML/CSS version, it displays a sample interface rather than generating real AI answers.

5. What are Nifty 50 and Sensex?

Nifty 50 tracks 50 major companies listed on the NSE, while Sensex tracks 30 major companies listed on the BSE.

6. What is the difference between CE and PE?

A Call Option (CE) gives the buyer the right to buy an underlying asset under specified terms. A Put Option (PE) gives the buyer the right to sell it under specified terms.

7. Does this website provide live market data?

No. This HTML and CSS project does not connect to live market data. A suitable market data integration would be needed to display current prices.

8. Can I calculate trading profit and loss?

The current version explains the profit and loss formula and displays calculator fields. Automatic calculation requires JavaScript or another suitable implementation.

9. Is trading risk-free?

No. Trading involves financial risk and losses are possible. Learning about risk management is important before making financial decisions.
