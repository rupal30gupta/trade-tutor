📘 Trading Glossary

Learn some commonly used financial terms.


1. Stock

A unit of ownership in a company.


2. Bull Market

A market condition in which prices generally rise.


3. Bear Market

A market condition in which prices generally fall.


4. Volatility

The degree to which a price fluctuates over time.


5. Portfolio

A collection of investments held by an investor.


6. Diversification

Spreading investments across different assets to reduce concentration risk.

🧮 Important Trading Formulas

Profit or Loss

(Selling Price − Buying Price) × Quantity


Percentage Return

(Profit or Loss ÷ Initial Investment) × 100


Risk-Reward Ratio

Potential Loss : Potential Profit


These are simplified educational formulas. Actual trading results may include brokerage, taxes, slippage and other charges.

💡 Tips for Beginners

  • Understand basic financial concepts first.
  • Learn about risks before trading.
  • Practise with hypothetical examples.
  • Avoid decisions based only on social media tips.
  • Never assume that profits are guaranteed.
  • Study the difference between investing and trading.
