01. Introduction to Stock Market

The stock market is a marketplace where shares of listed companies are bought and sold. A share represents ownership in a company.


Learn Market Basics →

02. Understanding Nifty 50 and Sensex

Nifty 50 tracks 50 major companies listed on the NSE, while Sensex tracks 30 major companies listed on the BSE.

03. Shares and IPO

Shares represent ownership in a company. An Initial Public Offering (IPO) is when a company offers its shares to the public for the first time.

04. Introduction to Derivatives

Derivatives are financial contracts whose value depends on an underlying asset or benchmark. Futures and options are examples.

05. Futures and Options

A futures contract creates an obligation under specified terms. An option gives its buyer the right, but not the obligation, to buy or sell the underlying asset.
