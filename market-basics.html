TRADING FUNDAMENTALS

Understand Market Basics

Learn how financial markets work before exploring advanced trading concepts.

1. What Is a Financial Market?

A financial market is a place or system where financial assets such as shares, bonds and derivatives are traded.

Financial markets help businesses raise capital and allow investors to buy and sell securities.

2. Who Participates in the Market?

Investors

Investors generally buy assets with the aim of achieving returns over time.


Traders

Traders buy and sell financial instruments according to their strategies and objectives.


Brokers

Brokers provide services that allow clients to place orders in financial markets.


Regulators

Regulators establish and enforce rules intended to support fair and orderly markets.

3. Types of Financial Markets

Equity Market

A market where company shares are traded.


Debt Market

A market for debt instruments such as bonds.


Derivatives Market

A market for contracts whose value depends on an underlying asset or benchmark.

4. Basic Order Types

Market Order

An order intended to execute promptly at the best available prices. The final price is not guaranteed.


Limit Order

An order to buy or sell at a specified price or a more favourable price. Execution is not guaranteed.


Stop-Loss Order

An order designed to help manage potential losses. Its execution price can differ from the trigger price in fast-moving markets.

5. Trading vs Investing

Trading often involves buying and selling over shorter periods. Investing commonly focuses on holding assets for longer periods.

Both approaches involve risk, and neither guarantees a profit.

6. Why Is Risk Management Important?

  • Understand the possibility of losing money.
  • Avoid risking money you cannot afford to lose.
  • Learn about diversification.
  • Understand transaction costs and taxes.
  • Practise with hypothetical examples before considering real trades.
